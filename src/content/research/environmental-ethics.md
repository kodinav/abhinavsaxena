Environmental ethics has long been organised by a debate over where moral concern should be centred: on human beings, on all living things, or on ecosystems as wholes.

My M.A. dissertation at the University of Delhi, *Psychocentrism: A Cognitive Reorientation of Environmental Ethics*, supervised by Dr Narmada Pujari, enters that debate. Environmental ethics is one of my areas of competence.
