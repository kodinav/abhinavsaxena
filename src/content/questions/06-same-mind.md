---
question: If a mind can be copied, paused and resumed, what is it for it to be the same mind?
order: 6
emphasis: ["copied", "same mind"]
areas: [philosophy-of-mind, existentialism]
experiment: ship-of-theseus-digital
concepts: [personal-identity, consciousness, mind]
---

Philosophers spent the twentieth century arguing about teleporters and split brains. Those cases are now operations: systems are checkpointed, forked and merged as a matter of course. Parfit argued that identity is not what matters in survival; if he was right, the question of whether a resumed system is "the same" may be less important than the question of what, in continuity, we actually care about.
