---
question: What happens to epistemic responsibility when knowledge is mediated by machines?
order: 3
emphasis: ["epistemic responsibility", "mediated"]
areas: [social-epistemology, ethics-of-ai]
experiment: epistemic-chain
concepts: [responsibility, testimony, trust]
---

We are responsible for what we believe in proportion to what we could have checked. When a belief reaches us through a chain — data, model, interface, summary — each link is somebody's or something's, and none is fully anyone's. Responsibility does not vanish in such chains, but it is redistributed in ways our ordinary practices of blame and trust were not designed to track.
