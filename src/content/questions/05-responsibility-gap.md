The standard worry is that autonomous systems open a "responsibility gap": no human controlled the outcome closely enough to be blamed, and the system is not the kind of thing that can be. I suspect the worry misdescribes the situation. Responsibility in these cases is not absent but distributed, and the philosophical task is to draw the distribution accurately enough that it can be acted on.
