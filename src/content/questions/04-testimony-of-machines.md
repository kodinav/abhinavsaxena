question: Is a model's answer testimony, evidence, or a third kind of thing?

When a person tells me the time, I acquire knowledge by testimony: I am entitled to believe her because she is a reliable speaker, and if she is wrong, she owes me an account. When a thermometer tells me the temperature, I acquire knowledge by evidence: the instrument owes me nothing. A language model sits uneasily between these. The classical Indian theory of testimony, which asks directly about the speaker's *standing*, may describe the case better than either.
