---
question: What does it mean to know something when the knower is an artificial system?

Every classical account of knowledge — justified true belief, reliabilism, virtue epistemology — was built around a subject who believes and can be asked why. A model has outputs that are often true, produced by a process that may be reliable, with no one who can be asked. My own work approaches the question through testimony, and suggests that what decides it is less whether there is a knower than whether the truth of what is said figures, through the source's own operation, in why it is said.
