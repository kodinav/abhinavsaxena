---
question: What does it mean to know something when the knower is an artificial system?
order: 1

Every classical account of knowledge — justified true belief, reliabilism, virtue epistemology — was built around a subject who believes and can be asked why. A model has outputs that are often true, produced by a process that may be reliable, with no one who can be asked. Either one of those accounts already covers the case, and we should be able to say which, or the concept of knowledge was quietly relying on a knower all along.
