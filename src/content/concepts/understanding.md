title: Understanding
definition: Grasping why something is so — the ability to connect, apply and extend a truth, rather than merely to state it.

relations:

    type: extends

  - to: ai
    type: challenges

"Does it really understand?" is the question everyone asks about language models, and it is usually asked badly. I try to separate the many things understanding can mean — semantic competence, explanatory grasp, practical know-how, the experience of insight — and ask about each in turn.
