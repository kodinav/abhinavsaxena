---
title: Trust
definition: Relying on someone or something while accepting vulnerability to their failure — a relation that is at once epistemic and moral.
order: 13
relations:
  - to: testimony
    type: grounds
    note: We accept testimony because we trust; trust is the enabling condition.
  - to: ethics
    type: extends
    note: Betrayed trust is a moral wrong, not merely a prediction error.
  - to: technology
    type: challenges
    note: Whether trust in a system is trust in the proper sense, or reliance dressed as trust, matters for what we can demand of it.
areas: [social-epistemology, ethics-and-technology]

I distinguish between reliance and trust. I rely on a bridge; I trust a friend. The interesting question is where artificial systems fall — and whether the vocabulary of trust, when applied to them, obscures the people behind them who can actually be trusted or not.
