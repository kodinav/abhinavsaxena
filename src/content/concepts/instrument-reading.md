Instrument reading is coming to know something by competently reading a reliable device, where the warrant is generated by the reader's use rather than transmitted from a speaker. A user reading a barometer comes to know the pressure when conditions are right: the instrument is well calibrated, she is competent with it, the conditions of use fall within those for which the calibration is valid, and she has reason to take all this to be so. No entitlement of interlocution, no assurance, no norm of assertion is in play. The epistemic load lies with her.

My positive proposal about language models is the Instrument-Reading Thesis. When a user comes to a warranted true belief that p on the basis of a model's output, her standing is that of a competent reader of an instrument: a function of the instrument's reliability in the conditions of use, her competence in operating and interpreting it, and the absence of defeaters. No warrant is transmitted from the system to the user; her warrant, when present, is generated by her use.

"Instrument" classifies the structure of warrant and is not a demotion. Instruments are genuine sources of knowledge, and perception itself has this structure. Nor does anything turn on the label: Freiman's technology-based beliefs, Duede's category between instruments and agents, and Alvarado's epistemic technologies are live alternatives, and the claim about warrant can be accepted under any of them.

Two differences from testimony follow. A hearer may defer, because her warrant runs through the speaker; an instrument reader cannot, and her appropriate attitude is calibrated reliance. And when an informant misleads, the hearer has social remedies (challenge, a demand for grounds, appeal to others), whereas when an instrument misleads the remedies are engineering ones.

Language models force a refinement: they are instruments without transparent calibration, and degenerate ones. A working barometer satisfies the [Tracking Minimum](/glossary/tracking-minimum/); these systems, on the best current evidence, do not. The record a user projects is all the connection there is (see [epistemic opacity](/glossary/epistemic-opacity/)).
