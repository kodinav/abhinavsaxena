I do not treat "artificial intelligence" as a settled kind of thing. It is a family of techniques, a set of institutions, and a philosophical provocation. The provocation is what interests me: a system that writes, argues, and plans confronts us with our own concepts — mind, agency, knowledge — and asks whether they were ever as clear as we assumed.
