---
title: Intentionality
definition: The aboutness of mental states — the way a thought can be of Vienna, or about tomorrow, or directed at a person who no longer exists.
order: 9
relations:

    type: grounds

    note: Whether a model's states are about anything, or merely correlate with things that are, is the modern form of the Chinese Room.
  - to: mind
    type: grounds
    note: Brentano called intentionality the mark of the mental; it remains the best candidate.
areas: [philosophy-of-mind, phenomenology]

Phenomenology made intentionality its central theme a century before language models existed. I think that tradition — Husserl's analyses of how meaning is constituted in acts — has more to say about machine "meaning" than most contemporary discussion admits.
