In 2004 Andreas Matthias argued that learning machines create a "responsibility gap": their behaviour is not sufficiently under the control of any human for that human to be blamed, and the machine is not a fit subject of blame either. The argument has structured the debate ever since.

Here you are given a scenario and a hundred units of responsibility. Allocate them. The instrument then shows how your allocation compares to the positions in the literature — and how much responsibility, on your own view, ended up nowhere.

**What to notice.** Most people leave a residue unassigned. Ask whether that residue is a genuine gap or a sign that you are looking for a single culprit where the correct answer is a distribution.
